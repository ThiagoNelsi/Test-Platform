# Question listing prototype

Question being answered: what could replace the current question listing UI across the personal bank and Explore pages?

Three directions are available from either route:

- `/questoes?variant=focus` — `focus`, Mesa de foco: a split reading desk with a queue on the left and one full question on the right.
- `/questoes?variant=shelf` — `shelf`, Catálogo editorial: a filter rail and dense editorial result rows.
- `/questoes?variant=radar` — `radar`, Radar de triagem: a difficulty board for scanning and batch selection.

The same variants are available under `/questoes/explorar`. All interactions use local demo data and are intentionally non-persistent.
